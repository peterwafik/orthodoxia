Holy Mass cards: the saints, second round

Every head is now covered by the monastic hood, and the beard is the boldest shape in each drawing. Same three ideas per saint; tell me which you want for each (for example “Cyril 2, Gregory 1, Basil 3”).

St. Cyril

Cyril 1 · Hood, Gospel and blessing

The black monastic hood with crosses covering the whole head, a thick full beard in two lobes, the omophorion with crosses, the Gospel book and a blessing hand.

Cyril 2 · Hood, staff and Theotokos medallion

The hood and full beard, the Coptic staff with its two serpent heads, a pectoral cross and a medallion of the Theotokos.

Cyril 3 · Hood, scroll and staff

The hood and full beard, an unrolled scroll for his writings, and the staff.

St. Gregory

Gregory 1 · Hood, Gospel and blessing

The hood with a veil falling behind, a short round thick beard, the omophorion, a decorated Gospel book and a blessing hand.

Gregory 2 · Hood and scroll

The same hood and beard, the omophorion with a pectoral cross and an unrolled scroll.

Gregory 3 · Hood, Trinity medallion and book

An open book and a medallion with the triangle of the Trinity, for “the Theologian”.

St. Basil

Basil 1 · Hood, open book and blessing

The narrow pointed hood, a long thick pointed beard, the omophorion, an open book and a blessing hand.

Basil 2 · Hood and staff

The pointed hood and long beard, a pectoral cross and the staff.

Basil 3 · Hood, Gospel and church medallion

The Gospel book and a medallion of a church for the great works he founded.
