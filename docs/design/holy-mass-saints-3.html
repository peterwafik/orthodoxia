Holy Mass cards: the saints, drawn after your reference

Drawn in the manner of the reference icon: a halo, wavy hair, a thick flowing beard, wide robes with fold lines and hands holding a book, with the face left empty. One for each saint.

St. Cyril

Monastic: long wavy hair, the cowl laid round the neck, a long pointed beard, holding a book with a cross.

St. Gregory

A bishop: a high bare brow with side hair, a thick round beard, the omophorion with a great cross, holding a stack of Gospels.

St. Basil

A monk-bishop: the hood covers the head, a long narrow beard, one hand raised in blessing and the other holding a scroll.
