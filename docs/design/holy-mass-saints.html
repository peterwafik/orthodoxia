Holy Mass cards: ideas for the three saints

Three ideas for each saint, still without facial features but with more detail: vestments, crosses, books, staffs and symbols. Tell me which one you want for each saint (for example “Cyril 2, Gregory 1, Basil 3”), or mix details between them.

St. Cyril

Cyril 1 · Hood, Gospel and blessing

Black Coptic patriarch’s hood with a band of crosses, a full beard, an omophorion with crosses, the Gospel book and a blessing hand.

Cyril 2 · Crown, staff and Theotokos medallion

The patriarch’s crown with a cross, the Coptic staff with its two serpent heads, a pectoral cross and a medallion of the Theotokos, since he defended that title.

Cyril 3 · Hood, scroll and staff

The hood and full beard, an unrolled scroll for his writings, the staff, and an omophorion with large crosses.

St. Gregory

Gregory 1 · Bare head, Gospel and blessing

The high bare head with a few side hairs, a short round beard, an omophorion with crosses, the decorated Gospel book and a blessing hand.

Gregory 2 · Phelonion and scroll

The same head, a bishop’s cape (phelonion) with its fold lines, the omophorion and an unrolled scroll.

Gregory 3 · Trinity medallion and open book

Called “the Theologian”: an open book, a medallion with the triangle of the Trinity, and the omophorion with crosses.

St. Basil

Basil 1 · Open book and blessing

The narrow face with side hair and a long pointed beard, the omophorion with crosses, an open book and a blessing hand.

Basil 2 · Phelonion and staff

The long beard, a bishop’s cape with fold lines, a pectoral cross and a staff.

Basil 3 · Gospel and the church medallion

The long beard, the Gospel book, and a medallion of a church for the great works he founded, with the omophorion.
