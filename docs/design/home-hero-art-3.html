Home page: minimalist drawings with the cross and Christ

Seven simpler ideas for the area beside the headline. Christ is drawn without any facial features: only the cross-marked halo, the shape of the head and the lines of the robe, so the drawings stay calm and quiet. All are in thin lines in the theme's accent colour, in light (default) and dark. They are sketches to choose a direction; a final drawing of Christ should be done or approved by an iconographer and reviewed by clergy.
