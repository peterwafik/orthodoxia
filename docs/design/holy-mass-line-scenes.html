The five Holy Mass scenes, redrawn in line art

The same five scenes as the film, now in the thin gold line of the site instead of clay dioramas: faceless figures, and as much detail as the line allows. If you like the direction, the next step is to turn them into the page background and the scroll transitions.

1. The sanctuary screen

The wooden iconostasis with its three curtained doors, the Last Supper and the apostles across the top, the Crucifixion above with the Virgin and St. John, the priest entering barefoot, deacons with candelabra, and his sandals left at the step.

2. The choir and the Word

The raised choir behind its lattice fence and three steps, the arcade with lamps and wall paintings, a deacon reading the Gospel at the lectern, cymbals and a triangle, candelabra, and the congregation with men on the left and women on the right.

3. The nave at the kiss of peace

The nave from the door: two colonnades with hanging lamps, men and women greeting each other with clasped hands, deacons with censers, and the priest lifting the cross at the open door of the sanctuary.

4. The haikal and the altar

The altar under its ciborium, a dome on four columns crowned with a cross, the priest with raised hands, the deacon with the censer, candles and the processional cross, and Christ enthroned among the four living creatures in the apse with Alpha and Omega.

5. Communion and heaven

Communion at the central door of the sanctuary, and above the church heaven opens: Christ enthroned with the Virgin and St. John the Baptist, the four living creatures, six-winged seraphim, angels with censers and the twenty-four elders. A symbolic meditation.
