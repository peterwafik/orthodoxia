Scene: Communion and heaven in a Coptic Orthodox church, drawn accurately as a sacred icon-style illustration, all figures fully clothed in long robes. Lower half: in front of the dark wooden haikal screen with ivory inlay and the crimson curtain of the open Royal Door with a hanging lamp, the priest in a white tunic and crimson-and-gold cross vestment holds the chalice and a gold spoon before a communicant who stands with arms crossed on the chest; deacons in white tunics with crossing stoles stand beside him, one with a censer and smoke, one with a candle; believers in long robes and veils stand in an orderly line with arms crossed on their chests between columns topped with crosses and hanging lamps. Upper half: heaven opens in a great arch of scalloped clouds with golden rays: Christ enthroned in a mandorla at the centre, raising a hand in blessing and holding a Gospel, with the Virgin Mary on one side and St John the Baptist on the other, both in prayer; around them the four living creatures (man, lion, ox, eagle) with many wings, six-winged seraphim, angels in long robes with censers in flight, and the twenty-four elders in two rows on each side wearing gold crowns and holding bowls of incense.
