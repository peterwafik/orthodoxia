Scene: The haikal (sanctuary) of a Coptic Orthodox church during the Liturgy of the Faithful, seen from the nave THROUGH the open Royal Door: the heavy crimson curtains and the dark carved wooden doorway with ivory inlay frame the view on both sides in the foreground. Inside, drawn accurately: the single free-standing altar in the middle of the sanctuary, a square table covered with a white cloth embroidered with red and gold crosses, standing under a ciborium (a small painted dome on FOUR slender columns crowned with a gold cross, with Christ painted inside the dome among stars). On the altar: the Gospel book, a gold cross, two tall candlesticks with lit candles, the veiled chalice and paten with a star cover. The OFFICIATING PRIEST in a white tunic and a crimson-and-gold cross vestment stands at the west side of the altar (nearest the viewer, seen from behind) facing east toward the altar with his hands raised in prayer, barefoot. Two other priests in white and gold vestments stand beside him on his right and left. Deacons in white tunics with crossing stoles stand around the altar on the north and south sides: one swings a censer with smoke, one holds a processional cross on a pole, one holds a candle, one holds a fan (marwaha) with a cross. Behind the altar the apse: a half-dome with Christ Pantocrator enthroned in a mandorla with Alpha and Omega, surrounded by the four living creatures (man, lion, ox, eagle) in medallions; below it the curved stepped stone seats of the priests (synthronon) with the bishop's throne in the centre. Ornamented rug and stone floor tiles.
