Scene: The khorus (the raised choir area between the nave and the haikal screen) of a Coptic Orthodox church during the Liturgy of the Word, drawn accurately. The haikal screen of dark wood with ivory inlay and its curtained doors forms the back wall, with hanging lamps and a star-patterned vault. The khorus is raised on three steps, with a carved wooden railing in front. A deacon reads the Gospel from a tall wooden lectern (manara) with an open Gospel book; deacons in white tunics with crossing stoles stand in two groups on both sides playing brass cymbals and a triangle (the only instruments of the Coptic Church), others chant holding hymn books; two seven-branch candelabra with burning candles. In front of the railing the congregation STANDS on the stone floor, men on the left and veiled women on the right with children, with a clear central aisle; there are NO pews. Warm lamp light, cream walls, columns with carved capitals topped with crosses.
