Scene: The kiss of peace in the nave of a Coptic Orthodox church, seen from the entrance door in one-point perspective, drawn accurately. Two colonnades of columns with carved capitals topped with crosses and arches, a beamed wooden ceiling, many brass hanging lamps down the aisle, framed icons on the side walls. NO pews: the faithful stand: men on the left and veiled women on the right turn to one another and clasp hands in peace, in several pairs at different distances. Two deacons in white tunics with crossing stoles walk down the central aisle, one swinging a censer with rising smoke, the other carrying a processional cross. At the far end the haikal screen of dark wood with ivory inlay and three doors; the central Royal Door is open with its crimson curtain drawn aside, and the priest in white and crimson-and-gold vestments stands in it lifting a cross, with two deacons beside him holding censers. Stone floor tiles in perspective and a long patterned carpet down the aisle.
