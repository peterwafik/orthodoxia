Scene: Communion and heaven, drawn accurately. Lower half: the Coptic church in front of the haikal screen (dark wood, ivory inlay, crimson curtain of the open Royal Door with a hanging lamp); the priest in a white tunic and crimson-and-gold cross vestment stands in the khorus before the Royal Door holding the chalice and giving communion with a spoon to a communicant; deacons in white tunics with crossing stoles stand beside him, one with a censer and smoke, one with a candle; believers stand in an orderly line to receive, with arms crossed on their chests: men on one side, veiled women and children on the other, between columns with crosses and hanging lamps. Upper half, above the church, heaven opens in a great arch of scalloped clouds with golden rays: Christ enthroned in a mandorla at the centre, blessing and holding a Gospel, with the Virgin Mary on one side and St John the Baptist on the other, both praying; around them the four living creatures (man, lion, ox, eagle) with many wings and eyes, six-winged seraphim, angels with censers in flight, and the twenty-four elders in two rows on each side wearing gold crowns and holding bowls of incense. A symbolic meditation, richly detailed.
