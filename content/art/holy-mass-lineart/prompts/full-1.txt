Drawing style: copy the two attached reference drawings exactly in their way of drawing. Clean black ink outline drawing of a Coptic Orthodox icon, like a coloring-book page: bold, even, smooth black outlines on a pure white background, no shading, no gray, no color, no photographic or 3D look, no clay. Rich in detail: every garment has flowing drapery folds, embroidered borders and crosses; every halo is a thin circle; hair and beards are drawn as many flowing lines; architecture has carved ornaments, rosettes, arches and crosses. Small solid black accents are allowed (for example the large crosses on a bishop's omophorion). IMPORTANT: every human and angelic face is left completely blank, a smooth empty oval with no eyes, no eyebrows, no nose and no mouth; hair, beards, ears, hands, halos and clothes are drawn in full detail. No text, no letters, no lettering anywhere, no frame, no border, no signature.
Scene: Preparation, seen from the nave. The full-width solid wooden Coptic iconostasis (screen of icons) with three arched doors, each with a drawn-back curtain tied with a tassel. Top row: the Last Supper in the centre with six apostles on each side, all with halos, seated behind a long table with a chalice and plates. Above it the Crucifixion with the Virgin Mary and St John, crowned with crosses on the posts. Lower row: arched icons of the Virgin with the Child, St Mark holding a Gospel, the Baptism of Christ in the Jordan with a dove, and Archangel Michael with a spear and wings. Carved rosettes and crosses on every post and panel. Two deacons in tunics with crossing stoles stand on the three-step raised area in front beside two tall candelabra; a priest in a phelonion with a cross, seen from behind, takes off his sandals and steps barefoot toward the central door; the sandals lie on the floor at the step. Stone floor tiles in perspective.
