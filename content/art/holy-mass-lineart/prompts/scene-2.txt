Scene: The Liturgy of the Word in the khorus (choir) of a Coptic church. The khorus is raised on three steps behind a lattice fence with a gap for the steps. Three tall arches with painted icons (the Virgin with the Child, Christ Pantocrator holding a Gospel, an archangel) and a star-patterned half-dome in each arch, hanging lamps, tall columns with carved capitals topped with crosses, a beamed ceiling with crosses. On the platform a deacon reads the Gospel at the central lectern with an open book; beside him deacons in tunics with crossing stoles play cymbals and a triangle; two tall seven-branch candelabra with burning candles. In front of the fence, on benches seen from behind, men sit on the left and veiled women on the right. Stone floor tiles.
