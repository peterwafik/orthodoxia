Scene: The haikal (sanctuary). In the centre the free-standing altar draped with an embroidered cloth, under a ciborium: a painted dome on four columns crowned with a large cross, Christ painted inside the dome among stars. On the altar two candles, a Gospel and a veiled chalice. A barefoot priest in a phelonion with a large cross on the back stands before the altar with raised hands, seen from behind; a deacon swings a censer on one side and another holds a candle on the other. Behind the altar the apse: a half-dome with Christ enthroned on a throne inside a mandorla with rays, Alpha and Omega beside Him (as simple Greek letters), and in four round medallions around Him the four living creatures: the man (angel), the lion, the ox and the eagle; stepped seats curve around the apse. In front, the carved wooden screens of the sanctuary with arched icons of the Virgin with the Child and of Christ, tied curtains, and crosses on the posts; a tall processional cross on a stand and a candelabrum; an ornamented rug on the floor.
