Subject: Liturgy of the Word. The raised khorus (choir area) on three steps with a low cross-shaped lattice fence, two wooden lecterns holding gold-bound lectionaries, two tall candelabra, rows of deacon seats. A deacon reads the Gospel at a lectern; deacons play brass cymbals and a metal triangle. Below the steps, wooden pews with men on the left and veiled women on the right. Twelve stone pillars, a whitewashed dome painted deep blue with gold stars and angels, hanging oil lamps, the iconostasis behind.
