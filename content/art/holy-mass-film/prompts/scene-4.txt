Subject: Liturgy of the Faithful, inside the haikal (sanctuary). A free-standing square altar under a ciborium: a small painted dome on four pillars with a large gold cross on top, the four evangelists painted on the pillars and Christ with seraphim in the dome. A barefoot priest in white and gold vestments at the altar with the covered chalice and paten under veils, a barefoot deacon with a censer, a standing gold cross, candles. Behind, the apse with Christ Pantocrator surrounded by the four living creatures (lion, ox, man, eagle) and alpha and omega; three steps in the apse niche up to Christ enthroned. The back of the iconostasis with curtains pulled open.
