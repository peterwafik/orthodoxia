Subject: Reconciliation and peace, the nave seen from the church entrance. A pointed-arch door with a cross in its keystone, a colonnade of twelve whitewashed stone pillars with hanging oil lamps, men on the left and veiled women on the right turning to one another and clasping hands in the kiss of peace, deacons with censers, the solid wooden iconostasis at the far end with its open central door and a priest holding a gold Coptic cross, the deep blue painted dome with gold stars above.
