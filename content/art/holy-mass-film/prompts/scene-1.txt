Subject: Preparation, seen from the nave. The full-width solid wooden Coptic iconostasis with three arched doors, each with a drawn-back curtain: top row the Last Supper in the centre with six apostles on each side, above it the Crucifixion with the Virgin Mary and St John; lower row icons of the Virgin with Child, St Mark, the Baptism, Archangel Michael. Two deacons stand on the raised three-step khorus in front of it beside two candelabra, a priest in white vestments takes off his sandals at the central door step, and small clay sandals stand on the floor.
