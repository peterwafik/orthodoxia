Subject: Communion and thanksgiving, a luminous ascent. At the khorus steps before the open central door of the Coptic iconostasis, a barefoot priest gives communion on a spoon from a gold chalice to a line of faithful (men, veiled women, children). Above the church a tall staircase of pearly clouds rises past angels with censers, six-winged seraphim, the four living creatures (lion, ox, man, eagle), and twenty-four crowned elders, to Christ enthroned in a gold mandorla with the Gospel in His left hand and His right hand raised in blessing, the Virgin Mary and St John the Baptist on each side.
