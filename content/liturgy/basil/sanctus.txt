en| Holy, holy, holy, indeed, O Lord our God. Who formed us, created us, and placed us in the paradise of joy When we disobeyed Your commandment by the deception of the serpent, we fell from eternal life and were exiled from the paradise of joy. You have not abandoned us to the end, but have always visited us through Your holy prophets. And in the last days You manifested Yourself to us, who were sitting in darkness and the shadow of death. Through Your only-begotten Son, our Lord, God, and Savior Jesus Christ, who, of the Holy Spirit and of the holy Virgin Mary.
